# One-paste art prompt: *The Curator* (museum theme)

Paste everything inside the box below into the image generator **once**. It makes sheet 1 and then waits;
reply **next** for each following sheet (10 in total), or reply with a correction to redo a sheet.

Save the sheets as `art/museum/sheet-01.png` to `sheet-10.png` in the order they arrive. The cell order and
grids match [museum-art-prompt.md](museum-art-prompt.md), which lists the file name for every cell.

HOW WE WORK
- Make SHEET 1 now.
- After each sheet, stop and wait. When I reply "next", make the next sheet. If I reply with a correction (for example "redo sheet 3: cell 7 should hold a quill"), remake that whole sheet with the fix, then wait again.
- Keep the style, line weight, lighting and palette identical across all 10 sheets.
- Every cell must be a separate, complete illustration. Follow the cell order exactly: the order is how the pictures are matched to cards, so never skip, swap, repeat or add a cell.

STYLE (applies to every sheet)
You are illustrating The Curator, a cosy strategy game set in a grand Victorian museum in London around 1890.
The look is a hand-drawn editorial illustration in the style of a Victorian museum catalogue: fine ink
outlines, soft watercolour washes, gentle paper grain, no photorealism, no 3D render look.

Palette: aged card stock #EEE2C7, ink navy #1E2A44, oxblood #7A1F2B, brass #C99A2E, and four
department colours used as accents: terracotta red #B3473C, sand gold #C99A2E, royal blue #3D6AA3,
museum green #4C8656.

Objects: every artefact is an invented museum piece (never a copy of a real, famous object), shown alone on a
small plinth or stand, three-quarter view, softly lit from the upper left like a museum photograph.
People: friendly, slightly whimsical Victorian museum staff, head-and-shoulders, three-quarter view,
holding the tool of their trade; every person is invented.

Contact sheets: always deliver a set as ONE image with a strict grid of equal square cells separated by thin,
even ivory gutters, filled left to right, top to bottom, in the order given. Each cell is a complete separate
illustration with its own flat background; nothing crosses into a neighbouring cell. Leave spare cells plain ivory.

Inside each cell: subject fills about 70% of the cell, centred, plain flat background in the requested tint.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, borders, frames, watermarks or logos anywhere on the sheet.
Each image must stay readable when shown at 64 px.
