# Kaiten

*A small-box worker-placement game about running a conveyor-belt sushi bar.*
2–4 players · about 30 minutes · ages 10+

Over 7 rounds you send chefs to stations around the restaurant, gather fish and
staples, take orders off the belt, and serve dishes. Served dishes stay on your
counter (your tableau). They score points, many keep working for you every
round after, and together they win over the regulars waiting at the door.

Playable version: open `kaiten.html` in a browser (you vs 1–3 bots). It fits one screen on phone, tablet and desktop.
Illustrations live in `art/` and are generated by `python3 tools/make_art.py art`.
Balance check: `node kaiten/sim.cjs 1000` plays bot-vs-bot games and reports
score spread, seat win rates and per-card win rates.

## Setup

1. Lay out the 7 stations. Cover the spots not used at your player count
   (see the table below).
2. Shuffle the fish deck and deal 5 face up as the **Fish Market**.
3. Shuffle the dish deck and fill the **belt**'s 5 slots, left to right.
4. Deal each player 2 dishes as **orders** (face up in front of you).
5. Each player takes 3 chefs (put the 4th, the apprentice, aside), 2 Rice,
   1 Nori and 1 Wasabi. Every player after the first takes 1 extra Rice.
6. Shuffle the regulars, deal **players + 4** of them into a face-down queue
   and return the rest to the box. Turn the top 3 face up at the door.

## A round

1. **Morning.** Dishes on your counter with an *Each round* effect pay out.
2. **Place chefs.** Starting with the first player and going clockwise, place
   one chef on an open spot and do that station's action right away. Keep
   going until everyone has placed all their chefs. If you have more chefs
   than the others, you keep placing after they run out.
3. **The belt turns.**
   - The dish in the *last lap* slot (far right) leaves; discard it.
   - Slide the remaining dishes right to close the gaps.
   - Put 1 Wasabi on every dish still on the belt.
   - Fill the empty slots on the left from the dish deck.
   - Everyone takes their chefs back.
   - If nobody used the Front Door, pass the first-player marker left.

After round 7, score the game.

## Stations

| Station | Action | Spots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish Market | Take 2 fish from the market, then refill it to 5. | 2 (3 with 4 players) |
| Pantry | Take 2 staples, Rice or Nori, any mix. | Unlimited |
| Kaiten Belt | Take up to 2 dishes off the belt as orders, with any Wasabi on them. | 2 (3 with 4 players) |
| Prep Counter | Cook 1 order. | Unlimited |
| Head Chef's Board | Cook up to 2 orders. | 1 |
| Front Door | Take the first-player marker for next round. Gain 1 Wasabi and draw 1 dish from the deck as an order. | 1 |
| Staff Room | Pay 2 Wasabi to hire your apprentice. They work from next round on (4 chefs). | 1 |

## Ingredients, orders and cooking

- **Rice** and **Nori** are staples and come from the Pantry.
- **Tuna, Salmon, Eel, Shrimp** are fish and come from the Fish Market.
  Spent fish go to a discard pile, which you shuffle into a new deck when the
  deck runs out.
- **Wasabi** is wild. When you cook, each Wasabi stands in for any one
  ingredient. You can also spend 2 Wasabi to hire your apprentice.
- You may hold at most **3 orders**. To **cook**, pay the ingredients printed
  on one of your orders and move it to your counter. Its effect starts now.
- You never cook straight off the belt. A dish must be an order first.

## Regulars

Three regulars wait at the door, each with a request such as *3 Nigiri* or
*3 dishes with Eel*. Right after your action, if your counter meets a face-up
regular's request, take that card and refill from the queue. You can win at
most one regular per turn. Regulars don't consume dishes, so one counter can
satisfy several over the game.

## Scoring

- **Dishes:** the VP on each plate.
- **Chef bonuses:** VP you collected from *each time you cook* effects.
- **Tasting sets:** 4 VP for each complete Nigiri + Maki + Sashimi trio on
  your counter. Specials don't count toward sets.
- **Regulars:** the VP on each regular you won.
- **Game-end cards:** effects that score at the end.

Most VP wins. Ties go to the player with more dishes served, then to the
player with more Wasabi left.

## Design notes

- **The belt is the clock and the catch-up.** A dish nobody wants grows more
  attractive each round as Wasabi piles onto it, so bad draws fix themselves
  and luck stays low.
- **Wasabi is the single flexible currency.** It smooths out the fish draw, and
  it pays for the apprentice, so you're always trading engine growth against
  cooking now.
- **Three pulls on every dish:** its plate VP, the set it completes, and the
  regular it might win, plus the engine effect printed on it.
- **Tuning from 1,000 simulated games per player count:** seat win rates are
  within about 3 points of even at 2, 3 and 4 players. Average scores run about
  53 VP (2 players), 48 (3 players) and 42 (4 players). An earlier free Staff
  Room gave the first player a 46% win rate at 3 players, which is why hiring
  now costs 2 Wasabi and nobody starts with more than 1.
